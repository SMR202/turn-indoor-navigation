# Next milestone: camera-assisted positioning, then passive corrections

Reviewed 2026-10-09. Research and implementation plan; no new positioning code, dependency installation or physical accuracy result. This refines the [October 4 proposal](ACCURACY_NEXT_STEP_2026_10.md), changing the first native experiment from iPhone-first to Pixel-first because TURN now has a locally buildable Android development host. It does not replace the camera-free product objective.

## Repository evidence

FACT: TURN has local venue loading, room-to-room routes, manual/QR starts, recorded doorway checkpoints, replay and button-triggered test uploads. Android build 2 preserves native sensor timestamps in batches; iOS uses Expo DeviceMotion. SDK 57 remains pinned. The user reports movement on both devices, with different calibration adjustments; this is qualitative feedback, not a measured build-2 accuracy result.

FACT: [NavigationEngine](../packages/positioning-core/src/engine.ts) still advances accepted steps by one fixed `stepLengthMetres` along phone yaw. [Calibration storage](../apps/mobile/src/navigation/calibration-storage.ts) retains a scalar per installation. Entering fewer steps for the same 5 m increases that scalar. It can compensate for missed/rejected detections without fixing them. Different devices need acquisition and estimator validation; changing phones does not itself change a person's physical stride.

FACT: routing does not correct the estimated position. There is no visual translation, radio or magnetic position correction, wall-aware estimator, floor inference or calibrated pose uncertainty. The [observation contract](../packages/contracts/src/index.ts) does not yet carry metric visual poses or coordinate-origin epochs. Some wall/door positions are plan-derived and need physical verification. A dot constrained to the selected route would conceal errors and mishandle a real wrong turn.

INFERENCE: further fixed-stride tuning can improve individual trials but cannot establish reliable room-to-room location. The next investment should add environmental evidence while preserving PDR as an available fallback and comparison.

## Technology choice and sequence

PROPOSED: make **camera-assisted room-to-room navigation** the next executable milestone. Adopt platform visual-inertial odometry (VIO): ARCore on Android, ARKit on iOS. These estimate metric device motion using camera features and inertial data. The existing 2D map can consume that position; AR arrows and 3D venue reconstruction are unnecessary for this milestone. [Google motion-tracking explanation](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/fundamentals), [Apple world tracking](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/understanding-world-tracking).

This is an experiment with a plausible accuracy benefit, not an accuracy promise. It introduces visual evidence beyond footfall counting, but shares inertial inputs with PDR; their errors are not independent. It also measures phone motion, not the person's torso. Pure vertical bobbing should not accumulate horizontal walking distance after correct projection; horizontal arm movement can still move the measured phone position and must be tested.

| Candidate                                  | Role                                                       | Decision now                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform VIO                               | Metric local translation without fixed stride              | First experiment; no purchased venue hardware. Camera must see useful features.                                                                                                 |
| Verified walls, doors and walkable areas   | Restrict plausible location hypotheses                     | Add after raw VIO comparison, with uncertainty and off-route behavior. Does not create an absolute fix.                                                                         |
| BLE plus PDR; magnetic survey where useful | Automatic building-referenced corrections with camera off  | Next product milestone. First benchmark zone/door discrimination, cross-device signals and survey repeatability; do not assume RSSI is accurate range.                          |
| Wi-Fi RTT                                  | Range to compatible access points                          | Optional Android extension, dependent on phone/AP capability and known AP locations. [Android documentation](https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/wifi/wifi-rtt). |
| UWB                                        | Precise relative ranging to compatible devices/accessories | Defer until a venue justifies hardware and device coverage constraints. [Apple Nearby Interaction](https://developer.apple.com/nearby-interaction/).                            |
| Learned inertial odometry                  | Potentially better camera-off dead reckoning               | Later benchmark; does not remove need for absolute corrections or cross-device held-out data. Existing reuse/license gates remain.                                              |

VIO is unsuitable in a pocket, with the camera covered, or where lighting/texture/motion prevents tracking. Foreground camera use has battery/thermal costs. It begins in an arbitrary local frame and can drift or reset. A single known start and direction can align a session for testing; QR identity alone does not measure orientation. Initial alignment remains explicit until a validated automatic venue-localization source exists. This stage does not deliver automatic initial location or eliminate all future re-anchoring.

The long-term product therefore remains **PDR + optional VIO + verified map constraints + passive location corrections**. For camera-off deployment, start with a small BLE correction pilot and compare a magnetic/vendor option; choose from measured performance and venue operating cost. Do not require repeated QR scans as the normal route experience.

## Reuse and compatibility findings

- **EXPERIMENT / ADOPT platform engine:** ARCore Android SDK 1.56.0, inspected release revision `3abfeb18669c2cbb2d07057f135d117ee9d91826`. Its [license file](https://github.com/google-ar/arcore-android-sdk/blob/3abfeb18669c2cbb2d07057f135d117ee9d91826/LICENSE) distinguishes SDK binaries governed by ARCore terms from sample-code licensing. Do not describe the whole runtime as Apache-licensed. A small local Expo module is compatible with our existing acquisition boundary; actual native integration remains unbuilt.
- **EXPERIMENT / ADAPT wrapper alternative:** ReactVision ViroReact 3.0.3, revision `75215cd8185d6e84bdeafef83fd7b468adb5af5a`, [MIT license](https://github.com/ReactVision/viro/blob/75215cd8185d6e84bdeafef83fd7b468adb5af5a/LICENSE). Its [package manifest](https://github.com/ReactVision/viro/blob/75215cd8185d6e84bdeafef83fd7b468adb5af5a/package.json) declares Expo 57 / RN 0.86 compatibility. However, inspected [camera event types](https://github.com/ReactVision/viro/blob/75215cd8185d6e84bdeafef83fd7b468adb5af5a/components/Types/ViroEvents.ts) expose position/orientation without native capture timestamp or origin epoch. It is a rendering-oriented option requiring further adaptation, not a selected drop-in measurement source. Native transitive licenses and actual builds are unverified.
- **PROPOSED preference:** use the platform engine through a narrow timestamp-preserving Expo adapter first, rather than add a rendering framework solely for camera position. Evaluate frame delivery and session resets before committing to the bridge. No SLAM implementation from scratch.
- ARKit comes from Apple's platform SDK; it has no independent npm version to pin. Record the Xcode SDK, device OS, app and adapter revisions. iPhone testing requires a signed native development build through a Mac or suitable build service; current Expo Go cannot gain this feature through a JS reload.
- Configure ARCore as optional and feature-detect device support, runtime installation and camera permission. Keep maps/PDR usable when unavailable. [Google setup requirements](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/java/enable-arcore).
- Material compatibility risk: an [Apple engineer acknowledged an ARKit tracking regression reported on LiDAR devices](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/827240). This does not establish that the user's iPhone is affected or that the issue persists on every current OS. Log exact OS versions and run a device smoke test; do not assume a Pro phone guarantees better VIO.

## Concrete implementation handoff

1. **Pixel acquisition slice:** add an optional camera-assisted mode in the existing SDK 57 development app. Acquire native ARCore pose, frame timestamp, tracking status/reason and session identity. Record exact device/runtime versions and delivery gaps. Give camera initialization, denial and unsupported-device states clear UI. Camera ownership must not conflict with the QR scanner.
2. **Contracts and replay:** version the visual-pose observation and session-to-venue transform. Preserve meters, gravity-aligned axes, orientation convention, native clock mapping and origin epochs. Never treat a reset/relocalization jump as walking. Store pose/quality data without camera images by default; old recordings keep their existing replay behavior. Synthetic transform tests must cover turns, vertical-only movement, delayed delivery, stale frames and resets.
3. **Usable route:** establish one measured start/direction, show camera-assisted position on the existing map, navigate a real destination, mark existing doorway checkpoints and save/upload the run with one button. Preserve a separate PDR comparison. Do not average poses or silently switch coordinates on failure. Keep uncertainty unknown until supported by evidence; show quality state separately. The existing PDR phone-tilt guard is not the validity rule for camera tracking.
4. **Physical comparison and iPhone port:** first establish viable Pixel tracking, then implement the same normalized source for ARKit and build the signed iPhone app. Compare both phones before selecting a fused estimator. Actual support and accuracy remain device tests, not outcomes of successful compilation.
5. **Map-aware fusion and passive correction:** use the measured results to choose quality gating, recovery and constraint models. Preserve raw and corrected paths. Then implement the BLE/magnetic pilot through the same observation boundary. This advances the existing roadmap's map-constraint and correction work, rather than tying positioning to a particular venue or screen.

Time-box the initial provider spike to two focused engineering days, excluding physical test availability/signing. At the gate, either deliver timestamped/replayable camera motion with safe reset handling or record a concrete blocker and evaluate the alternate adapter. Do not spend weeks adding AR visuals before evaluating translation.

## Evaluation and exit gate

PROPOSED pilot: verify one multi-turn route and its door distances physically, then collect at least three normal and three brisk repetitions per phone, spread across sessions and both directions. Capture VIO and PDR together where their carry assumptions permit; report when camera posture invalidates PDR comparison, and retain separate PDR baseline walks. Reserve sessions for evaluation rather than tuning.

Include standing still, vertical bobbing, horizontal phone handling, camera covered, blank floor/wall, lower light, stop/restart and app interruption. Report each device separately: checkpoint error before/after the still wait, turn lag, endpoint error, false horizontal displacement, tracking availability, recovery discontinuity and failed runs. Phone checkpoint taps are approximate timing labels; they are not a surveyed continuous ground-truth trajectory.

Advance only if held-out doorway errors and turn timing improve over PDR without unacceptable false motion or discontinuities. The existing provisional 95% within 1 m doorway target is a product goal, not a pass inferred from a few walks; publish sample counts, worst cases and failures. A VIO-only failure should narrow its supported mode, not lead to forced route snapping. No new physical tests or automated accuracy analysis were performed during this research review.
