# ADR-0016: Room blocks and a camera director for the 3D map

Date: 2026-11-14
Status: ACCEPTED
Context and evidence: The first MapLibre style (ADR-0013) extruded every wall segment to 2.4 m. Reviewed in the browser on 2026-11-13, it read as a maze of dark slabs: rooms were empty holes without names, tall walls hid rooms and the route, the building floated in a void, and the camera turned with a lagging compass. The owner's brief asked for a map that looks like a building and moves well while walking. Apple, Mappedin and Pointr draw places as low volumes and circulation as open floor.
Decision: Rooms are low solid blocks (1.8 m) with their name under a category badge; corridors, halls and outdoor areas are open floor; wall segments are low rims (0.9 m) that show only where no block covers them. The room the walker stands in, the rooms a route passes through and the destination show their floor instead of a block (`openRoomIds`); a selected place lifts in the accent colour. A blurred line under the floor gives the building a ground shadow. While a route is followed, a pure camera director (`followCamera` in `@turn/map-scene`) decides the view: **course-up** (the direction to a point 7 m ahead on the route, never the compass), tilt, scale and where the dot sits, in three phases (cruise, manoeuvre within 8 m of a turn or floor change, arrive within 6 m). Renderers ease towards it every frame with a turn-rate limit. A route preview is framed whole and turned so it reads bottom to top.
Alternatives considered: Keeping thin extruded walls at a lower height: still no sense of place, and rooms stay unnamed. Heading-up from the compass: unreadable when the magnetometer swings indoors. Per-screen camera code in each renderer: two diverging behaviours and nothing testable.
License/platform implications: None new; the same MapLibre style runs on web and phones. Phones keep the blocks in their own source because a source inside the style cannot be updated alone.
Consequences and risks: Automatic conversions can produce rooms that overlap corridors; a block then hides floor. MapLibre draws lines over extrusions, so the route can appear to cross a block's top at shallow angles; blocks are kept low. The phone renderer has not yet been run on a phone.
Validation / revisit trigger: Unit tests for the director, blocks and open rooms; a headless-browser walk on the demo venue. Revisit after the first phone session and the first FAST floor on the new style.
Supersedes / superseded by: Refines the styling part of ADR-0013.
