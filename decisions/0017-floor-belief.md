# ADR-0017: Floors from a belief over barometer, beacons and the route

Date: 2026-11-14
Status: ACCEPTED
Context and evidence: Floor changes waited for the walker to tap "I'm on Level 1". Phone barometers resolve 0.01–0.05 hPa after a second of smoothing and one storey is about 0.43 hPa, but weather drifts pressure by about 1 hPa an hour, doors and ventilation produce steps of 0.1–0.3 hPa, and many budget Android phones have no pressure sensor at all. Published results with a barometer alone are mixed (65% floor accuracy in one six-building study). A concrete slab weakens a beacon by 15–20 dB.
Decision: Floor is a discrete belief (a probability per floor), never a single sensor's reading. `FloorChangeDetector` turns pressure into events using only the change of height since the last level stretch: a change of at least 60% of a storey that then stays level for 2.5 s counts, the storeys moved are rounded from the height, the climb rate and stepping classify stairs, lift or escalator, changes that complete in under 1.2 s are treated as pressure steps and ignored, and slow drift is followed only while level. `FloorBelief` shifts by barometer events, is weighted by the strongest beacon heard on each floor (beacons on other floors are listened to only for this), gets a small pull from the route at stairs and lifts, and is set by the walker's confirmation or a scanned code. The shown floor changes when another floor exceeds 0.8; the confirmation button stays as the fallback. After a change the 2D filter restarts at the far end of the nearest stairs or lift.
Alternatives considered: Barometer threshold only: wrong on phones without the sensor and in buildings with pressure steps. Absolute altitude against a reference: weather makes it useless without a reference station. Beacons only: slower, and blind where landings have no beacon.
License/platform implications: `expo-sensors` Barometer, already in the builds; iPhones report about once a second.
Consequences and risks: Thresholds are untuned defaults; buildings with pressurised stairwells or fast lifts will need recordings. The beacon planner should always propose a beacon at each stair and lift landing.
Validation / revisit trigger: Simulated traces only so far (drift, landings, lifts, pressure steps, mezzanines). Revisit with the first recorded floor changes on a phone; target is a wrong floor shown for more than 10 s in at most 1 of 20 changes.
Supersedes / superseded by: None.
